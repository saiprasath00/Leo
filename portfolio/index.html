How I work

Three habits I picked up from building the TCS model.

  1. Read the statement first.

    Start with the P&L and the notes, not the chart. The story is in the detail.

  2. Make the assumption visible.

    Every driver should be editable. If I can’t change it, I can’t test it.

  3. Check the total.

    A model is only as good as its checks. Then design the view that answers the question.
